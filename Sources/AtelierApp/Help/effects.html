Post-Crop Vignetting

Style
Highlight Priority (default) darkens like lower exposure but keeps bright areas bright. Color Priority keeps colors as they are while darkening. Paint Overlay mixes the edges toward black or white: flat and soft.
Amount
Negative darkens the edges and draws the eye in; positive lightens them for a soft, airy look.
Midpoint
How far toward the middle it reaches. Lower reaches further in.
Roundness
At 0 the vignette has the crop's proportions. Positive makes it rounder, toward a circle; negative squarer, toward a rounded rectangle.
Feather default 50
How soft the edge is. 0 gives a hard edge.
Highlights
With a negative Amount in Highlight or Color Priority, brings bright areas in the darkened corners back, such as a lamp or the sun.

Glow

Light spilling from the bright parts of the photo. Not in Lightroom Classic (Camera Raw and Luminar have one).

Style
Diffusion: a soft, dreamy haze, like a diffusion filter. Bloom: highlights spill light onto what is around them. Halation: the red-orange glow film shows around bright lights, as on CineStill 800T.
Amount
How strong the glow is. 0 is off.
Range default 50
Which tones glow. Higher lets darker tones glow too.
Spread default 50
How far the light reaches, as a share of the photo's size, so a preview and an export match.
Warmth
Tints the glow warmer (positive) or cooler (negative).

Grain

Film-like grain. It changes brightness, not color, and is strongest in the midtones, so pure black and white stay clean.

Type default Standard
The kind of film the grain comes from. Standard is Lightroom-like grain. Fine: the small, tight, crisp grain of slow film such as Ektar or Velvia. Cubic: the larger, sharp-edged, clumpy grain of classic film such as Tri-X, HP5 or Kodak Gold, reaching further into the shadows. Tabular: the smooth, soft, even grain of modern T-grain film such as Portra or T-Max, with cleaner shadows and highlights. Each type is equally strong at the same Amount. The built-in film presets pick the type that fits their film.
Amount
How strong the grain is.
Size default 25
Grain particle size. Above 25 the photo is softened slightly, so large grain replaces fine detail as with real film.
Roughness default 50
0 gives even, regular grain; higher clumps it and varies its strength across the frame.
