Finishing touches: a vignette that follows your crop, and film grain.

Post-Crop Vignetting

Amount
Negative darkens the edges and draws the eye in; positive lightens them for a soft, airy look.
Midpoint
How far toward the middle it reaches. Lower reaches further in.

Grain

Film-like grain. It changes brightness, not color, and is strongest in the midtones, so pure black and white stay clean.

Amount
How strong the grain is.
Size default 25
Grain particle size. Above 25 the photo is softened slightly, so large grain replaces fine detail as with real film.
Roughness default 50
0 gives even, regular grain; higher clumps it and varies its strength across the frame.
