Remove an object, replace a background or enlarge a photo with AI models running on your own Mac. The result is saved as a new photo stacked on the original, which never changes.

Set up ComfyUI

Atelier sends photos to ComfyUI, a free app you run yourself; nothing is bundled with Atelier.

  1. Start ComfyUI (the desktop app, or from Terminal).
  2. In Atelier › Settings… › AI, set Server: the ComfyUI desktop app uses http://127.0.0.1:8000, ComfyUI from Terminal uses port 8188. Click Connect.
  3. Pick the Edit model and the Upscale model (4×) from what your server has, and a Generation size: 768 px (faster), 1024 px, or 1536 px (slower).

The AI panel shows whether ComfyUI is reachable. If it says “ComfyUI not reachable”, start ComfyUI and click Check.

Open the AI panel

In Develop, click the wand (the third icon under the histogram), or choose Photo › Remove Object…, Replace Background… or Upscale…. Pick a Recipe, set it up, then click Generate. Progress shows as “Generating… N s” with a bar and a percentage; Cancel stops it.

Remove Object

  1. Choose Remove Object.
  2. Paint over what should go. Brush Size (or [ ]), Paint or Erase, and Clear to start again.
  3. Optionally say what it is (“a man”, “a sign”), which helps the model.
  4. Click Generate.

Replace Background

Atelier finds the subject (the background to replace is tinted on the photo), and you describe the new background in words. The subject's pixels are kept exactly.

Upscale

Enlarge 2× or 4× with an upscale model, for big prints or heavy crops.

Results

Each result is a 16-bit TIFF named name-AI Recipe.tif next to the original, stacked on it with a ✦ AI badge in the grid. Atelier records the recipe, models, licenses, prompt and seed with it, so any result can be traced and reproduced.

Licenses: what you can publish

For your website or shop, use only results from permissively licensed models (Apache 2.0 or MIT), such as Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 (the default for Remove Object and Replace Background) or FLUX.2 [klein] 4B. Non-commercial models such as Qwen-Image-2.1, Fibo 2.0 or FLUX.2 [klein] 9B are for experiments only.

Your own workflows

Save a ComfyUI workflow in API format as remove-object.json, replace-background.json or upscale.json in the folder from Settings › AI › Show Workflows Folder, and it replaces the built-in one. Title its nodes Atelier Image, Atelier Mask, Atelier Prompt, Atelier Sampler and Atelier Output, as in the example files there.
