Export

Save finished photos as JPEG, HEIC, TIFF or PNG, with your edits, at the size and color space you need, with your watermark if you want one. Save the choices you use often as presets. Originals are never changed.

Open the Export dialog

Select photos and choose File › Export… (⇧⌘E), click Export in the toolbar, or right-click › Export…. In Loupe and Develop it exports the photo you're on (or the filmstrip selection).

  1. Presets fill in everything on the right in one click (see Export presets). The preset that matches the current settings is highlighted; change any setting and the highlight goes, as in Lightroom.
  2. Format: JPEG and HEIC (with Quality, 50 to 100), TIFF (16-bit) or PNG.
  3. Resize to fit: the long edge in pixels. Off keeps the full size.
  4. Color space: sRGB for web and social, Display P3 or Adobe RGB (1998). Sharpening: None or For screen.
  5. Metadata: All metadata, All except location (removes GPS), or None.
  6. Add watermark puts your name or logo on every photo (see Watermark).
  7. Save to a folder. Atelier remembers your last choices.

Export presets

  • Atelier has three built-in presets: Website (JPEG 85, 2048 px long edge, sRGB, sharpened for screen, no location), Full-Size JPEG (JPEG 95, all metadata) and Full-Size TIFF (16-bit, Adobe RGB, for print or further editing). Hover over a preset to see what it sets.
  • To save your own, set everything up and click + under the list, then name it. A preset keeps the format, quality, size, color space, sharpening, metadata and watermark. It doesn't keep the folder.
  • Right-click a user preset for Update with Current Settings or Delete…. To delete the highlighted preset you can also click −. Built-in presets can't be changed.
  • Using the name of a preset you already have replaces that preset.
  • User presets are shared by every catalog.

Watermark

Turn on Add watermark to see its settings, with a preview of the first photo above them showing exactly where the watermark will go.

Style
Text (it starts as © and the year with your name; type anything) in White or Black, or Image: click Choose… and pick a logo. A PNG with a transparent background works best.
Size
The watermark's width as a share of the photo's width. Because it's a share, the watermark looks the same on a 2048 px website JPEG and a full-size TIFF.
Opacity
100% is solid; lower lets the photo show through.
Position
Click one of the nine dots: a corner, an edge or the center.
Inset
The gap between the watermark and the photo's edges, as a share of the photo's short side.
Shadow
A soft drop shadow that keeps white text readable on bright skies.

Double-click a slider's name to reset it. The watermark is drawn after resizing and sharpening, so it stays crisp and is never sharpened. If the text is empty or no image is chosen, Export stays dimmed until you fix it or turn the watermark off.

While it runs

  • Exports run in the background, with progress and Cancel in the status bar. Keep working meanwhile.
  • Files keep their original names. An existing file is never replaced: a number is added instead. A virtual copy is named name-Copy1.jpg.
  • Keywords, title, caption and rating are written into the files (unless Metadata is None).
  • Photos whose drive is offline are listed under Show Problems; the rest still export. A photo on a NAS that's offline still exports from its copy on this Mac, if there is one, and Show Problems notes that it couldn't be checked.
  • Photos on a NAS are read through the cache of originals, once each. One whose file changed on the NAS since import isn't exported; a sheet at the end lets you choose for it (see When the original changed).
