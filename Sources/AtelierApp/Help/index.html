Welcome

Atelier catalogs your photos and edits them without ever changing the originals. Search with the field on the left, or ⌘F.

From card to website in four steps

  1. Import a shoot: ⇧⌘I, or drag a folder onto the window.
  2. Cull to pick the keepers from each burst, then rate and flag with single keys (1–5, P, X).
  3. Develop (D): start with Basic, then crop, mask and grade.
  4. Export (⇧⌘E) with the Website preset.
