Transform

Straighten leaning buildings, trees and artwork. Upright finds straight lines and fixes the perspective for you; Guided lets you draw the lines yourself.

Upright

Off
No Upright correction.
Auto
Like Full, but holds back when the lines are weak, so it never warps a photo hard on thin evidence. A safe first try.
Level
Makes horizontal lines level. Rotation only.
Vertical
Also makes vertical lines parallel. The usual choice for buildings and trees.
Full
Also corrects horizontal perspective, for a wall shot from an angle.
Guided
You draw the lines. See Guided.

If there aren't enough straight lines, the button says “Not enough straight lines found” and nothing changes. The correction is saved with the photo; click the mode again to measure again.

Upright resets the crop. As in Lightroom, choosing an Upright mode resets the crop's cut and straighten angle and the manual Transform sliders (rotations and flips stay). ⌥-click the button to keep them.

Guided

Click Guided or press ⇧T, then drag along edges that should be straight up or straight across.

  1. Drag along a tree trunk or building edge to draw a guide. Each guide counts as vertical or horizontal, whichever it's closer to.
  2. The photo straightens once there are two guides, and updates with each guide you add or move (up to four). Two vertical guides fix leaning verticals; add horizontal guides to fix the horizontal perspective too.
  3. While you draw or move a guide's end, a 3× loupe beside the pointer shows exactly where you are. Show Loupe or O turns it off.
Drag a guide's endMove it.
Click a guide, then DeleteRemove it. Clear Guides removes all of them.
⌘⌥ drag the photoMove it with X and Y Offset (add ⇧ to move in one direction only).
Esc or DoneClose Guided.

A strong correction can push part of the photo out of the frame, guide ends included. Lower Scale to bring it back, or turn on Show Whole Photo to zoom the view out to the whole corrected photo. That changes only the view, never the edit.

Beyond Lightroom. Show Whole Photo isn't in Lightroom, and every guide ends exactly straight even with four guides.

Manual sliders

Added on top of Upright, for fine-tuning or for doing it by hand.

Vertical −100–100
Negative widens the top: fixes buildings shot from below.
Horizontal −100–100
Turns the photo left or right, for a wall shot from the side.
Rotate −10°–10°
Small rotations.
Aspect −100–100
Positive stretches the width, negative the height. Fixes buildings that look too tall after a correction.
Scale 50–150%
Shrinks or enlarges the corrected photo in its frame.
X Offset, Y Offset
Moves the photo sideways or up and down.

Constrain Crop

Perspective corrections leave empty, light gray areas at the edges. Turn on Constrain Crop to crop to the largest rectangle with no gray, keeping your crop's shape. It finds the crop again whenever the correction changes, and the Crop tool won't let the crop leave the photo. Turning it off leaves the crop as it is.
