Set up

Settings

Open Atelier › Settings… (⌘,). Changes take effect at once.

Pick a pane in the sidebar on the left; its settings show on the right. The sidebar groups the panes: Atelier (General, Appearance, Keyboard), Photos (Import, Develop, Export, Metadata) and Editors & AI (Edit In, AI). The sidebar has the keyboard when Settings opens: ↑ ↓ move between panes. After you click into a pane, click the sidebar again to get the arrows back (the chosen row turns from gray to blue). Settings reopens on the pane you used last. Click a section's header band to close or open it, as with the panels in the main window; Atelier remembers which are closed.

Keyboard

The Keyboard Map at the top shows each key with a small picture of its command: stars for ratings, label colors, flags and tool symbols. Choose ⇧ Shift, ⌃ Control or ⌥ Option to see the keys used with them. Hover a key to see its command; click it to jump to that command in the list. Search commands or keys at the bottom finds a command by name, group or key.

Every keyboard command (ratings, flags, labels, views, tools, Masking, Guided Upright, and ⌃Tab between areas) with its key. Click a key and press a new one, with ⌃, ⌥ or ⇧ if you like; a key moves from any command that had it. Arrows, Tab, Return, Esc, Delete, F6 (Show Filmstrip) and ⌘ shortcuts stay fixed. Delete removes a key, Esc cancels, Restore Defaults goes back to Lightroom's keys. Help › Keyboard Shortcuts (⌘/) lists them all; its Change Keys… button opens this pane.

Metadata

Automatically write changes into XMP sidecars, including current Develop edits, masks, retained history and snapshots for Atelier recovery. Other apps see the standard metadata, such as ratings, keywords and captions. See XMP sidecars.

Edit In

Each external editor has its own section: the app, its name in the menu, the file it is sent (TIFF or JPEG), the color space and whether develop adjustments are included. The − in a section's header removes that app; Add App… and Find Installed Editors are at the bottom. See Edit In.

AI

The ComfyUI server and Connect, the edit and upscale models, generation size, and the workflows folder. See AI tools. Masking Models shows the people model built into Atelier and lets you download or remove the two Fine Edges models (Fast, about 115 MB; Hair Detail, about 470 MB) and choose which one runs (one run takes about 18 GB of memory for Fast and 40 GB for Hair Detail; on a Mac with less, Settings says so under the model, and if memory runs out the run stops with a message instead of slowing Atelier), and the depth model for Depth Range (about 50 MB).

Agents

Chat apps (OpenCode, Cherry Studio, Ollama with ollmcp, LM Studio, Claude, Codex) can work with your library through Atelier's agent connection (MCP). With Look they search photos, read details, Develop settings and cull groups, and list the menu commands. With Organize they can also select photos in Atelier, rate, flag, label, add or remove keywords, set titles and captions, add photos to collections, make virtual copies, and run library commands. With Edit they can also edit in Develop: set or nudge sliders (Exposure, Contrast, White Balance, HSL, Color Grading, Detail, Effects…), run Auto and Auto White Balance, apply a Develop preset, sync settings from one photo to others, reset settings, crop, straighten, rotate and flip, add masks (Select Subject, Select Background, Select Object, Select People, Linear and Radial Gradients, Luminance Range; Brush and Color Range stay yours), match a look to a photo or a built-in study, and run Develop commands. Exports and other apps come in a later version, and no app can delete anything.

When an app edits in Develop

Each edit is one step in the photo's History, named for the app, such as Exposure +0.50 (Claude), so you can step back past it as with your own edits. The notice at the bottom of the photo offers Undo, which adds an Undo … step that puts the settings back, but only while nothing has changed them since. If you're dragging a slider or handle when the app asks, Atelier finishes your drag first. Changing a slider in a panel that's switched off turns the panel on, as when you do it. A value past a slider's end stays at the end, and White Balance Temp can't be nudged on a photo that's As Shot (the app can set it instead). Develop menu commands an app runs, such as Develop › Rotate Left, act on the photo open in Develop.

Auto and Match Look look at each photo's pixels first. While they do, the status bar at the bottom of the window shows the app and how far it has got, such as Claude: Auto Tone, 3 of 12, with Cancel; cancelling changes nothing. A photo you edit while it measures is left as you made it. A mask an app adds shows in the Masks panel like yours, and a crop in the crop tool, so you can adjust either by hand.

When an app changes photos

Each change shows a notice at the bottom of the photo, such as Claude: Rate 3 stars (37 photos), with Undo for about ten seconds. The app can undo its own change too. Undo works only while the change is still the latest on those photos: if you, or another app, changed them since, Atelier keeps that later work and says why it can't undo. Undo puts back the rating, flag, label, keywords, title, caption and collections; a new collection stays (empty), and virtual copies are removed with Photo › Remove Virtual Copy…. A change to more photos than Ask before one change touches more than allows asks you first, in Atelier's window.

Several changes at once

An app can send several changes in one go, such as “label the picks green, rate them 4 stars and add them to Portfolio”. Normally each change is its own, with its own notice and Undo, and the app stops at the first one that fails. An app can also ask for all or nothing: then the whole batch is one change, with one notice and one Undo, and its Develop edits are one step in each photo's History, named for the batch. If any part fails, Atelier puts back what the batch already did and changes nothing. All-or-nothing batches can rate, flag, label, add keywords, add to collections and edit in Develop with the app's own tools; Develop menu commands and virtual copies have to be sent one by one. When a batch touches more photos than Ask before one change touches more than allows, Atelier asks once, for the whole batch.

Photos in the chat

A chat app that shows app views, such as Docent, shows the photos an app finds as a strip of cards in the conversation, with each photo's stars, flag and color label. Move with the ‹ and › buttons or the arrow keys, and click N more at the end to load the next photos. Select a card and click Show in Atelier (or double-click it, or press Return) to select it in Atelier's window; that needs Organize or more. Ask about this photo sends a message about it to the chat for you. The pictures appear only while Let agents see photo previews is on; otherwise each card says No preview. Private photos never appear. Chat apps without app views, such as Cherry Studio and OpenCode, show the same results as text.

Docking beside a chat app

A chat app built for it, such as Docent, can line Atelier's window up beside its own: Docent stays on the left and Atelier fills the right, the same height. Only the chat app's own window code can do this; no model can move Atelier's window. While docked, Atelier keeps the photo and collapses side panels when there isn't room: the left panel goes first, then the right one, and they come back as soon as the window is wide enough again. Drag Atelier's window away by its title bar, or resize it, to undock it (the chat app is told). Undock in the Access section does the same. When the chat app undocks Atelier itself, it either leaves the window where it is or puts back Atelier's own size and place. Atelier's window docks at 520 points wide or more, and not in full screen.

Access
Allow chat apps to use Atelier (on by default; turning it off disconnects every app). Let agents see photo previews (off): when on, an app can look at a photo as a JPEG up to 1024 px. Include location in photo details (off). Ask before one change touches more than 10, 25, 50 (the default), 100, 250 or 500 photos, or Never ask. Private photos are never shared, whatever View › Show Private Photos says. While a chat app has docked Atelier's window, a line here says so, with Undock.
Apps
Every app that has connected, with a green dot while it is connected and the menu of what it may do: Don't Allow, Look, Organize, Edit or Everything. The first time an app connects, Atelier asks in its own window and you pick one there. Changing it disconnects the app, which reconnects with the new access. The − forgets the app, so Atelier asks again next time. Apps started from Terminal are listed as Terminal.
Connect an App
The helper's path with Copy, and Copy Setup for the app chosen in Setup for, ready to paste where the line under it says. When Atelier runs from Xcode, the helper appears after you build the atelier-mcp scheme once (Product › Scheme › atelier-mcp, then Product › Build).
Activity
What each app did, newest first: connected, allowed, each tool it called and any error. Click a line to select it (⌘-click adds lines, ⇧-click selects a range); the copy button copies the selected lines, or the whole log when none is selected, oldest first with date and time. Right-click a line for Copy and Copy All. The trash button clears the log.
