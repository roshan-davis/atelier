Lens Corrections

Undo what the lens did: bent lines, dark corners and colored edges. Most of it is one checkbox.

Remove Chromatic AberrationProfile correctionsDistortionDefringeVignetting

Remove Chromatic Aberration

Removes red-cyan and blue-yellow edges that grow toward the corners. There are no sliders: Atelier measures how far the red and blue parts of the image are out of line in this photo and lines them up. Turn it on for most photos.

Enable Profile Corrections

Fixes the lens's own distortion and dark corners using the correction data your camera writes into the RAW file (Nikon Z cameras do this). It's on by default for RAW files, and the panel shows the lens's name. When there's no data for a photo (or it isn't a RAW), the checkbox is dimmed with “No lens corrections for this photo”.

Distortion

For anything the profile doesn't fix, or a lens with no profile. Positive removes barrel distortion (straight lines bowing outward); negative removes pincushion (lines bowing inward).

Defringe

Removes purple and green fringes along high-contrast edges, mostly from shooting at wide apertures. A fringe pixel loses its color but keeps its brightness.

Purple Amount, Green Amount 0–20
How much to remove. Higher reaches further from each edge.
Purple Hue, Green Hue
Which shades count as fringe, on a strip of the colors it covers. Drag an end to widen or narrow the range, or the middle to move it. Keep Green narrow so foliage is safe.

Fringe Color Selector

  1. Zoom in on a fringe (double-click the photo).
  2. Click the eyedropper next to the Defringe heading. You can still zoom and move around.
  3. Click right on the purple or green fringe. Atelier centers that color's Hue range on it and turns its Amount up to 5 if it was 0. Anything else shows a message and the eyedropper stays out. Esc puts it away.

Hold ⌥ while dragging: Amount shows white with only the fringes being removed, in their color; Hue shows black outside the range, so you can see which colors it covers.

Only colors in a Hue range that sit near a strong edge change, so purple flowers or a green shirt away from edges keep their color.

Vignetting

Brightens (positive) or darkens the corners of the whole, uncropped photo, to undo the lens's dark corners. Midpoint sets how far in from the corners it reaches. For a creative vignette around a crop, use Effects › Post-Crop Vignetting.
