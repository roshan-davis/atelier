Sharpening

Amount 0–150
How much to sharpen. Start around 40 to 70.
Radius 0.5–3.0
How wide the sharpened edges are. Low for fine detail (feathers, landscapes), higher for soft subjects.
Detail 0–100, default 25
Below 25 holds back halos on strong edges; above 25 brings out fine texture.
Masking 0–100
Keeps sharpening to edges. 0 sharpens everywhere; 100 only the strongest edges, so skies and skin stay smooth.

Noise Reduction

Luminance
Smooths grainy speckle in brightness (high ISO). Too much looks plasticky; stop when the grain is calm.
Color
Removes colored blotches in shadows and flat areas. A little goes a long way.

Order. Noise reduction runs before sharpening, so reduce noise first, then sharpen with some Masking so the noise isn't sharpened back.
