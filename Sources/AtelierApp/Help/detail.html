Sharpening

Amount 0–150
How much to sharpen. Start around 40 to 70.
Radius 0.5–3.0
How wide the sharpened edges are. Low for fine detail (feathers, landscapes), higher for soft subjects.
Detail 0–100, default 25
Below 25 holds back halos on strong edges; above 25 brings out fine texture.
Masking 0–100
Keeps sharpening to edges. 0 sharpens everywhere; 100 only the strongest edges, so skies and skin stay smooth.

Noise Reduction

AI Denoise 0–100
Takes the noise out of a RAW photo with a neural network built into Atelier, keeping fine detail far better than Luminance. See AI Denoise below.
Luminance
Smooths grainy speckle in brightness (high ISO). Too much looks plasticky; stop when the grain is calm.
Color
Removes colored blotches in shadows and flat areas. A little goes a long way.

AI Denoise

For high-ISO and underexposed RAW photos. Drag AI Denoise up from 0; 50 to 70 suits most photos, 100 takes out all the noise it finds. Its spread is measured on each photo, so it adjusts itself to the camera and ISO.

  • The first time on a photo, the line under the slider says Finding the noise… for a few seconds while the photo is worked on at full size. The photo shows Atelier's usual noise reduction until it's done; after that every Amount is instant.
  • While AI Denoise is above 0, the RAW engine's own noise reduction is off, so the slider starts from the photo's real noise. Use Luminance and Color on top if you want it smoother still.
  • It works on RAW files only. On JPEG, TIFF and HEIC photos the slider does nothing and the line under it says so.
  • Everything runs on your Mac (Core ML where it can), nothing is downloaded or sent anywhere, and the original stays untouched. Exports and previews wait for it.

The model. PMRID (Wang et al., ECCV 2020, Apache License 2.0), about 4 MB, built into the app. Its results are fine for your website or shop.

Order. Noise reduction runs before sharpening, so reduce noise first, then sharpen with some Masking so the noise isn't sharpened back.
