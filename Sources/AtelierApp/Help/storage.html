Storage locations

Atelier has no cloud of its own. You add the places you already keep photos (a NAS, an external drive, a folder, a cloud drive folder such as Dropbox) in Atelier › Settings… › Storage, and Atelier uses them. Nothing is set up until you add it.

Add a location

  1. Open Atelier › Settings… › Storage and click +.
  2. Choose Drive or Folder on This Mac, Network Share (NAS) or Cloud Drive Folder, then Continue.
  3. Pick the place: a drive or folder in the Open panel, a server found on your network or an address such as smb://megham.local/Photos, or a cloud drive found on this Mac.
  4. Check the name (it starts as the share's or folder's name) and click Add Location.

You can add only a folder on a drive, for example Photos SSD/Portfolio. Photos Atelier already has from that drive stay exactly where they are; a location never moves or renames anything.

Backups to S3-compatible storage (Backblaze B2, Cloudflare R2, Amazon S3, MinIO) come in a later update.

A NAS

  • Servers that share over SMB on your network are listed by name. Double-click one, or select it and click Connect.
  • macOS asks for the user name and password the first time, in its own window, and keeps them in your Keychain. If you saved the password in Finder before (Remember this password in my keychain), nothing is asked. Atelier never stores a password itself.
  • Connect when Atelier opens (on by default for a share) connects it at launch, quietly, with the Keychain's password. If the NAS is off, Atelier opens as usual and the status bar says it is offline.

Cloud drive folders

iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, Box, Synology Drive and other cloud drives work through their own Mac app, which keeps a folder in sync. Atelier never signs in to them; it reads the files in that folder.

  • The cloud drives on this Mac are listed by name. Select one and click Use All of Dropbox (or double-click it), or Choose Folder… to pick only the folder where you keep photos.
  • Photos are remembered by cloud drive, not by where the Mac keeps its folder, so signing in again or moving to a new Mac keeps them linked.
  • A photo that is still only in the cloud shows a cloud mark on its thumbnail. Its preview shows as usual; when you open it in Develop, Atelier asks the cloud drive's app to download it, shows the download in the status bar with Cancel, and you can edit as soon as it arrives. Nothing is downloaded just by browsing.
  • The Import dialog says how much it will download before you import from a cloud folder, for example “412 photos · 18.4 GB · 11.6 GB will be downloaded”.
  • If the cloud drive's app signs out or is removed, the location shows Signed Out and its photos stay browsable from their previews.

Details

Name
What the sidebar's Locations and the Storage pane call it. Press Return to keep a new name.
Where
The folder on this Mac while it is connected.
Connect · Disconnect
Mount or unmount a share. Also in the sidebar: right-click the location under Locations, or click the small Connect button on an offline share's row.
Show in Finder
Opens the location's folder.

When a location is offline

Its photos stay in the grid from their previews, with the offline mark, and you can still rate, flag and keyword them. Library › Refresh Volumes (⌘R) checks again after you connect it some other way.

What stays on this Mac

The catalog and every preview always stay on this Mac, whatever locations you add, so browsing never waits on the network and a sync app can never damage the catalog.

Remove a location

Select it and click −, or Remove Location… in its details. Its photos stay in the catalog (offline while the drive isn't there), and nothing on the location is changed.
