FaceMesh.mlpackage, FaceBlendshapes.mlpackage and FaceDetector.mlpackage

Contain Google's MediaPipe Face Landmarker models (Face Mesh V2, Blendshape V2 and
the BlazeFace short-range face detector;
face_landmarker.task, float16, SHA-256
64184e229b263107bc2b804c6625db1341ff2bb731874b0bcc2fe6544e0bc9ff), from
https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/face_landmarker/face_landmarker/float16/latest/face_landmarker.task
Copyright Google LLC. Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (see
FaceLandmarker-LICENSE.txt). Model cards: Face Mesh V2, Blendshape V2 and
BlazeFace Short Range (Apache 2.0).

Changes made for Atelier: the three TFLite graphs (face_landmarks_detector.tflite,
face_blendshapes.tflite and face_detector.tflite) were rebuilt op by op as Core
ML programs, the mesh and detector models' weights stored in 16-bit floating
point and the mesh model's outputs flattened. Made by Scripts/models/convert_mediapipe_face_landmarker.py in the
Atelier repository.
