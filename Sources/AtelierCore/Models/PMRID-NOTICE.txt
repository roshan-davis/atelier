PMRID.onnx

Contains the pretrained weights of PMRID ("Practical Deep Raw Image Denoising on
Mobile Devices", Yuzhi Wang, Haibin Huang, Qin Xu, Jiaming Liu, Yiqun Liu and Jue
Wang, ECCV 2020), from https://github.com/megvii-research/PMRID
(models/torch_pretrained.ckp, SHA-256
9361614f3514d27351d81909f2215c0fdc38619c0288d936b7266485ac106c14).
Copyright Megvii Inc. Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (see
PMRID-LICENSE.txt).

Changes made for Atelier: the network (models/net_torch.py) was rebuilt as an
ONNX graph with a fixed 1 x 4 x 512 x 512 input, holding the checkpoint's weights
unchanged. Made by Scripts/models/convert_pmrid_onnx.py in the Atelier repository.
