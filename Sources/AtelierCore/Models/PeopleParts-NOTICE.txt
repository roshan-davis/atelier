PeopleParts.mlpackage

Contains Google's MediaPipe Multiclass Selfie Segmentation model
(selfie_multiclass_256x256, float32, SHA-256
c6748b1253a99067ef71f7e26ca71096cd449baefa8f101900ea23016507e0e0), from
https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/image_segmenter/selfie_multiclass_256x256/float32/latest/selfie_multiclass_256x256.tflite
Copyright Google LLC. Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (see
PeopleParts-LICENSE.txt).

Changes made for Atelier: the TFLite graph was rebuilt op by op as a Core ML
program, the weights stored in 16-bit floating point, and a softmax added after
the model's output. Made by Scripts/models/convert_mediapipe_multiclass.py in the
Atelier repository.
